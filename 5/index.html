1.2.3: I also tested the UNet with pure noise and got this result:

I noticed over multiple rounds that the output picture would vaguely look like an 8 or 0. I think, following the idea of centroids, that it has to do with what the model is expected to do and the data it's been given. Given pure noise, the model tries to predict the safest number, and since the noise is uniform everywhere it would make sense to predict a number or shape that's as spread out as possible. In the case of MNIST, those numbers would likely be 0 or 8, which is why the output shape looks like a roundish blob or an 8.
